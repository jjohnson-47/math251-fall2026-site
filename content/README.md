# Course content source

Use this directory for instructor-owned, public-safe source material that will be rendered into the student site.

Before adding content:

- confirm it belongs in the public companion rather than the course LMS;
- remove student information and private platform links;
- identify any date or policy that needs current LMS verification;
- preserve equations in accessible text or MathML-friendly source; and
- record asset provenance and usage rights.

Do not place raw Blackboard, Pearson, mailbox, roster, gradebook, or accommodation exports here.
