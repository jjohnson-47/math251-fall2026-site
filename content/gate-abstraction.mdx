        The stopwatch already contained the derivative.
      </h2>

      Call the time $t$ and the distance so far $f(t)$. Gate A is at time $t$;
      Gate B is at $t+h$. Then

      $$
      \Delta d=f(t+h)-f(t),\qquad \Delta t=h,
      $$

      so the readout is

      $$
      \frac{\Delta d}{\Delta t}=\frac{f(t+h)-f(t)}{h}.
      $$

      Squeezing the gates is making $h$ small. At the 25-metre mark, the
      averages approach $10\ \text{m/s}$ even though the two gates never occupy
      the same place:

      $$
      f'(t)=\lim_{h\to 0}\frac{f(t+h)-f(t)}{h}.
      $$

      <p className="math-caption">
        Nothing new happened; the stopwatch got a notation. A limit means
        “squeeze until the answers stop moving.”
